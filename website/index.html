FAQ

Is Nectan secure?

Yes, absolutely. Your data is fully encrypted end-to-end using industry-standard protocols.

Is Nectan free to use?

Yes, Nectan is free to use.

Is Nectan fast?

Yes. Nectan tries to fully saturates your network connection, ensuring your files are transferred as fast as possible.

What platforms are supported?

Nectan currently offers full support for Windows, Linux, and Android devices.

Are there any limitations?

Absolutely not. You can send dozens of documents, massive 4K video files, games, or directory backups. If your hard drive can store it, Nectan can transfer it.
